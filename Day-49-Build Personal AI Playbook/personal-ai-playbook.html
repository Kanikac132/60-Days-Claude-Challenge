What this tool does

This is a personal system for reusing your best AI prompts instead of rewriting them from scratch every time. It has four working parts: Workflows are ready-made, editable prompt templates for common tasks. The Prompt Builder lets you assemble a custom prompt from small labeled pieces (like "Role" or "Tone") and see it come together live. The Loop Builder turns any prompt into a self-checking, multi-step instruction the AI can iterate on until it meets your bar. Saved Library holds everything you've favorited, built, or customized — stored only in this browser.

What is the Personal AI Playbook?

A quick orientation — reopen this anytime from the "What is this?" button.

This tool helps you stop rewriting the same AI prompts from scratch. Instead of a list of prompts to memorize, it gives you reusable building blocks you combine differently for each task. Everything you build or favorite is saved automatically in this browser.

The four sections
  • Dashboard — an overview of your saved work and quick links into the other sections.
  • Workflows — ready-made templates for common tasks (writing, coding, research, planning). Each has fill-in variables, an example, and tips.
  • Prompt Builder — assemble a custom prompt piece by piece (role, context, format, tone, etc.) with a live preview.
  • Loop Builder — turns a prompt into a self-checking loop by adding a goal, a way to judge quality, and rules for when to stop.
Where things are saved

Everything — custom workflows, saved prompts, saved loops, favorites, and settings — is stored in your browser's local storage on this device only. Nothing is sent anywhere. Use Export in Settings to back it up or move it to another browser.

The Loop Builder

For tasks that need real iteration, the Loop Builder adds a goal, quality checks, and stop conditions — so the AI keeps refining its own answer instead of stopping after one try. Everything you make is saved automatically in this browser.
