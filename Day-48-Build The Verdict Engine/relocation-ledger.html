How this was researched

On accuracy: this tool was built by an AI assistant without live internet access at generation time. Every figure is the assistant's best recollection of real, named public datasets (C2ER, Tax Foundation, NOAA, Walk Score, Apartment List) rather than a fabricated placeholder — but recollections of specific index values can drift or go stale. Figures flagged est. are the most likely to have moved since this was written; figures flagged stable are structural facts (like a state's tax code) that change rarely. Before using this for an actual move, verify current numbers at the linked sources.
